We are delighted to announce that 5 sets of chambers have now signed up to the FreeBar Charter as of 22 September 2021.

[Gatehouse Chambers](https://gatehouselaw.co.uk/), [Lamb Chambers](https://www.lambchambers.co.uk/), [Matrix Chambers](https://www.matrixlaw.co.uk/), [St Ives Chambers](https://www.stiveschambers.co.uk/) and [Spire Barristers](http://spirebarristers.co.uk/) have all signed up to the Charter.

Please feel free to get in touch with any of the signatories if you have questions about the process. The key contacts from each organisation can be found below. Alternatively, please e-mail [info@freebar.co.uk](mailto:info@freebar.co.uk) and a member of the team will get back to you in due course.

[Click here](/resources/the-freebar-charter/) to learn more about the Charter.
